FITMENT KNOWLEDGE CENTER

CHOOSING THE
RIGHT SIZE

Understanding proper wheel fitment is critical for performance, safety, and aesthetics. Each vehicle type requires specific diameter, width, and PCD configurations for optimal handling and clearance.

🚗

Compact Hatchback

Swift, i20, Baleno, Polo

Diameter 15"–17"
Width 6.0J–7.0J
Common PCD 4×100, 5×114.3

City efficiency priority. Smaller diameters maintain ride comfort over rough roads while keeping unsprung weight low for responsive handling.

🚙

Compact SUV

Creta, Seltos, Ecosport, Nexon

Diameter 17"–18"
Width 7.0J–8.0J
Common PCD 5×114.3

Balances sporty handling with ground clearance. 17" provides best ride comfort; 18" enhances steering precision for highway driving.

🏎️

Luxury Sedan

BMW 3/5, Mercedes C/E, Audi A4

Diameter 18"–20"
Width 8.0J–9.0J
Common PCD 5×120, 5×112

Performance-tuned fitment. Wider contact patch improves high-speed stability and cornering grip. German brands use unique PCD patterns—exact matching required.

🚐

Full-Size SUV

Fortuner, Endeavour, Pajero Sport

Diameter 18"–20"
Width 8.0J–9.0J
Common PCD 6×139.7

Load rating critical for 7-seater payload. 6-bolt PCD distributes stress across more mounting points. Verify load index matches or exceeds OEM spec.

🛻

Off-Road 4×4

Thar, Jimny, Gurkha, Scorpio

Diameter 17"–18"
Width 7.5J–8.5J
Common PCD 5×139.7

Moderate diameter preserves tyre sidewall flex for obstacle absorption. Lower offset (ET 0-20) prevents rubbing on suspension articulation during rock crawling.

🚙

Luxury SUV

Range Rover, X5, GLE, Cayenne

Diameter 20"–24"
Width 9.5J–11.0J
Common PCD 5×120, 5×130

Statement fitment for air-suspended platforms. 22"+ requires low-profile UHP tyres. Forged monoblock construction mandatory for weight savings at this scale.

Need expert fitment advice for your specific vehicle? Our Karol Bagh team offers precision measurement and compatibility verification.

TYRE KNOWLEDGE CENTER

PERFORMANCE
CATEGORIES

Understanding tyre performance categories helps you match compound characteristics to your driving style and vehicle requirements.

🏙️

Everyday City

Long Tread Life Fuel Efficient Quiet Ride Wet Grip
Who It's For Daily commuters, city hatchbacks, light sedans
Typical Sizes 165/80R14 to 205/60R16
Example Vehicles Swift, i10, City, Verna
Brands Available:

Apollo, MRF, Goodyear

Why Choose This Category

Budget-friendly construction with proven durability for Indian road conditions. Optimized tread compounds deliver excellent fuel economy and extended mileage without compromising wet-weather safety.

🛣️

Premium Touring

Low Road Noise Ride Comfort All-Season Highway Stability
Who It's For Highway cruisers, luxury sedans, family SUVs
Typical Sizes 205/60R16 to 235/55R18
Example Vehicles Creta, Harrier, Camry, Octavia
Brands Available:

Yokohama, Bridgestone, Michelin

Why Choose This Category

Balanced performance for mixed city and highway driving. Advanced silica compounds provide superior wet braking while multi-pitch tread blocks minimize highway drone for a refined cabin experience.

🏁

Performance Sport

High-Speed Stability Cornering Grip Responsive Steering Track-Capable
Who It's For Enthusiast drivers, sport sedans, hot hatches
Typical Sizes 225/45R17 to 245/40R19
Example Vehicles BMW 3-Series, Mercedes C-Class, GTI, Civic Type R
Brands Available:

Yokohama (Advan), Bridgestone (Potenza), Michelin (Pilot Sport)

Why Choose This Category

Track-capable compound formulation with reinforced shoulder blocks for minimal flex under cornering loads. Reduces understeer, shortens braking distances, and maintains consistent grip through temperature cycles.

🚙

Luxury SUV / 4×4

High Load Rating Puncture Resistance Off-Road Tread Reinforced Sidewalls
Who It's For Full-size SUVs, luxury crossovers, off-road vehicles
Typical Sizes 265/65R17 to 285/45R22
Example Vehicles Fortuner, Thar, Range Rover, GLE, X5
Brands Available:

Yokohama (Geolandar), Bridgestone (Dueler), BFGoodrich (All-Terrain)

Why Choose This Category

Reinforced sidewalls resist punctures from rocky terrain while aggressive tread evacuates mud and snow. High load-index rating maintains stability under full payload and towing loads on highway or trail.
